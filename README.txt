PATIL UJJWAL COACHING CLASSES — MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE PACKAGE
============================================================

WHAT'S INSIDE (real, separate pages — proper routing, not one long page)
- index.html              -> Home (hero, stats, about preview, how-it-works steps, courses preview, why choose us, testimonials slider, CTA)
- about.html               -> About Us (full story, our approach, why choose us)
- courses.html             -> Courses (full 6-program grid)
- results.html             -> Results & Testimonials
- gallery.html             -> Gallery (masonry grid + lightbox)
- contact.html             -> Contact (info cards + enquiry form)
- privacy-policy.html      -> Placeholder legal page
- terms-conditions.html    -> Placeholder legal page
- assets/css/style.css     -> One shared stylesheet used by every page
- assets/js/script.js      -> One shared script (navbar, animations, counters,
                               testimonial carousel, gallery/lightbox, form validation)
- assets/images/logo.png   -> Your original uploaded logo (used as the actual brand mark
                               throughout — favicon, navbar, hero, footer)
- original-logo/           -> Your original uploaded logo file, kept for reference

HOW TO PUBLISH
1. Upload the WHOLE FOLDER (keep the assets/ subfolders exactly as they are) to your
   web host root (e.g. public_html) — index.html will load as your homepage and the
   navbar will link correctly to the other pages.
2. Free options: drag-and-drop the folder into Netlify Drop, or push it to a GitHub
   repo and enable GitHub Pages (root = this folder).

WHAT TO CUSTOMISE BEFORE GOING LIVE
- Stats numbers on the Home page (Years of Excellence, Students Guided, etc.)
- Course cards on courses.html — replace the 6 sample subjects with your real batches
- results.html — add your verified board results, milestone numbers and real testimonials
- gallery.html — open assets/js/script.js, find "galleryData" near the top, and swap
  each placeholder tile for a real <img> if you'd like actual photos instead of tiles
- contact.html — address, phone, email, working hours (appears here and in the footer
  of every page)
- privacy-policy.html / terms-conditions.html — replace placeholder text with your
  actual policy
- The enquiry form currently only shows a success message in the browser; connect it
  to your email or a form service (e.g. Formspree, Google Forms, or your own backend)
  to actually receive submissions.
